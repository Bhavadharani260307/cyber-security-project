Why Accuracy Alone Is Not Enough

In network intrusion detection, dataset class distribution is heavily imbalanced (e.g. 99% benign traffic, 1% stealthy infiltration).

A naive dummy model predicting "BENIGN" for every single packet would achieve 99% raw accuracy while failing to detect 100% of cyber threats!

Therefore, evaluation relies on Recall (Sensitivity) to minimize False Negatives and F1-Score to balance precision against stealth attacks.
